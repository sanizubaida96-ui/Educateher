# EducateHer 🌍🎓

**EducateHer** is an open-source web platform designed to dismantle cultural barriers and empower women through education. It provides community advocacy toolkits, outreach materials, an interactive impact simulator, and flexible learning pathways to challenge the practice of prioritizing early marriage over female education.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Interactive Impact Simulator:** Dynamically visualizes how increasing female education rates boosts household income, child health, and community literacy.
- **Community Dialogue Toolkit:** Ready-to-use radio scripts, religious/community leader briefs, and workshop outlines with one-click copy functionality.
- **Mobile-First & Lightweight:** Pure HTML, Tailwind CSS, and Chart.js—optimized for fast loading on mobile devices and low-bandwidth networks.
- **Open Advocacy Framework:** Information on legal minimum age of marriage protections, scholarship resources, and flexible re-entry policies for young mothers.
Interactive Impact Simulator
​What it is: A dynamic visual tool (like an interactive calculator or dashboard) where users can move sliders or change numbers to see real-time projections.
​What it does: It demonstrates the ripple effect of educating girls by showing data-driven outcomes:
​Household Income: How secondary or higher education directly increases future earning potential.
​Child Health: How educated mothers lead to reduced infant mortality rates, better child nutrition, and higher vaccination rates.
​Community Literacy: How educating women raises overall community literacy levels across generations.
​Purpose: Uses data visualization to convert abstract arguments into compelling, concrete evidence for policymakers, donors, and community members.
​2. Community Dialogue Toolkit
​What it is: A collection of practical, field-ready communication materials for grassroots campaigning.
​Key Components:
​Radio Scripts: Pre-written messages for local radio broadcasts, reaching areas with low internet access or high illiteracy rates.
​Leader Briefs: Talking points tailored specifically for religious and tribal/community leaders to help them frame girls' education within cultural and religious values.
​Workshop Outlines: Step-by-step guides for facilitating local town hall meetings or youth workshops.
​Key Feature ("One-click copy"): Allows field workers or advocates to quickly copy templates to their clipboard or messaging apps (like WhatsApp) for fast sharing.
​3. Mobile-First & Lightweight (Tech Stack)
​What it is: The technical design specification ensuring the website works smoothly in remote or low-connectivity areas.
​Tech Stack Breakdown:
​Mobile-First: Designed to look and function perfectly on smartphones, which are often the primary device used in target communities.
​Pure HTML & Tailwind CSS: Avoids heavy server processing or complex frameworks, allowing web pages to load instantly even on slow 2G/3G networks.
​Chart.js: A lightweight JavaScript library used to render the interactive graphs in the Impact Simulator without slowing down the page.
​4. Open Advocacy Framework
​What it is: A legal and resource repository providing actionable knowledge for girls, parents, and local activists.
​Key Areas Covered:
​Legal Minimum Age of Marriage: Clear information on local and national laws protecting children from child marriage.
​Scholarship Resources: A curated list of financial aid, stipends, and grants available to keep girls in school.
​Re-entry Policies: Guidance on policies that allow young mothers or married girls to return to formal schooling without stigma or legal barriers.
